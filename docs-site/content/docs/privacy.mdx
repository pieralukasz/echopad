---
title: Privacy
description: What EchoPad records, where it goes, and what never leaves your Mac.
---

- **Audio** is recorded only between start and stop, into `~/Library/Application Support/EchoPad/Library` and, if you choose, your save location.
- **Transcription and speaker detection** run on your Mac, on the Neural Engine, through [FluidAudio](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio).
- **Network:** the only requests download the speech and speaker models from Hugging Face during setup. After that EchoPad works offline.
- **No analytics, crash reporting or account.** The source is open, so you can check.

## Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations differ by country and state. Many require everyone’s consent. Tell people you are recording; EchoPad does not do it for you, because it never joins the call.
