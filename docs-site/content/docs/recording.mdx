## Start and stop

- **Menu bar:** click the icon, then **Start Recording** or **Stop**. Right-click the icon to start or stop without opening the panel.
- **Shortcut:** **⇧⌘E** by default. Change it in [Settings → General](/docs/settings#general). It works in every app and needs no Accessibility permission.
- **Window:** the **Record** button at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Notification:** see below.

Recordings shorter than 1.5 seconds are discarded, so a double press does not leave empty notes.

## Meeting detection

When Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet in a browser, Slack, FaceTime, Webex, Discord, WhatsApp, Signal or Telegram starts using the microphone, EchoPad shows a notification with a **Record** button. Recordings started this way stop by themselves when the call ends.

Turn it off in **Settings → General → Notify me when a call starts**.

## What gets recorded

EchoPad writes two tracks on one timeline:

| Track | Source | Labelled as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microphone | The input device you picked | Your name |
| System audio | Everything your Mac plays, only the call app, or apps you pick | Speaker 1, Speaker 2… |

Because your voice comes from its own track, it is always attributed to you, even when the other side talks over you. When the other side plays through your speakers, the microphone hears them too; EchoPad drops microphone words that match the system track at the same moment, so they are not credited to you twice.

<Callout type="info">Wear headphones if you can. The transcript is cleaner when the microphone hears only you.</Callout>

## The recording pill

<PillStage />

The pill floats above other windows at the bottom of the screen. While recording it shows a red dot, the elapsed time, a waveform and a stop button (with live transcript on, also a caption button that opens [the live transcript panel](#the-live-transcript-panel)). After you stop it shows progress, then **Saved**.

- **Move:** drag the pill anywhere. EchoPad remembers the spot, also on other displays. Drop it near its old place at the bottom and it snaps back.
- **Close:** hover over the pill and click the **×** in its corner. The recording keeps going; stop it from the menu bar or with the shortcut. The pill comes back for the next recording, and a problem message still shows.
- **Reset:** right-click the pill and choose **Move Back Above the Dock**.

Hide it for good in **Settings → General → Show the floating recording pill**.

If a detected call stays silent on the system track for 20 seconds, a yellow speaker appears in the pill. Click it to open the sound check in the menu bar panel. It shows whether the System Audio Recording permission is allowed and which apps are recorded, with a button to fix each problem. **Play Test Sound** plays a short sound on your Mac and listens for it: if EchoPad hears it, capture works and the other side is just quiet. The test sound ends up in the recording.

## The live transcript panel

With **Live transcript** on (Settings → Transcription), the pill shows a caption button while
recording; it is also **Show Live Transcript** in the menu bar panel. It opens a small floating
window with the words so far, labelled with who spoke (you, or Speaker 1, Speaker 2… on the system
track). Grey words are a draft: the model may still change them, and they turn black in place when
they settle.

- The window stays above other windows but never takes the keyboard from the app you are in.
  Closing it does not stop or pause the recording or the live transcript; open it again any time.
- A pause of 2 seconds or more starts a new paragraph; the name and time show only when the speaker
  changes (hover over a paragraph to see its time). When two people talk over each other, each
  keeps their own paragraph instead of the words alternating one by one, and a short "yes" or "no"
  in between does not split the other person's sentence.
- It follows the newest words while you are at the end. Scroll up to read earlier lines and it
  stays where you are; **Resume Following** jumps back to the end.
- In a long recording it shows the last 300 lines; the whole live transcript is still kept and the
  local API returns all of it.
- It shows when the model is still loading, when live transcript is off for this recording, and
  why it failed. After Stop it keeps the live text until the next recording; the saved transcript
  is made separately by Parakeet.

## If transcribing gets stuck

After Stop the pill shows the transcription steps. When a step makes no progress for too long
(loading the speech model: 3 minutes without download or loading progress; each transcription step:
2 minutes plus half the recording's length), EchoPad stops waiting: the conversation shows
**Failed** with the reason and **Transcribe Again**, and the audio stays in the library. The next try
loads a fresh speech model instead of waiting behind the stuck one. A stuck model cannot be
interrupted, so its memory is only given back when EchoPad quits. A long recording that keeps
making progress is never cut off.

## Importing audio

**Import Audio** in the Conversations toolbar takes any audio or video file macOS can read (m4a, mp3, wav, mov, mp4…). It is transcribed with speaker detection and saved like a recording.
