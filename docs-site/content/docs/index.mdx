EchoPad is a free, open source meeting recorder for macOS. It records your microphone and the other side of the call as two separate tracks, transcribes them with NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v3 on your Mac, tells the speakers apart, and writes the transcript into a folder you choose.

<Screenshot name="conversations" alt="The EchoPad window with a transcript split by speaker" caption="Every recording is kept in EchoPad and saved to your folder." />

EchoPad lives in the menu bar. Start a recording from there, with **⇧⌘E**, or from the notification that appears when a call starts. A small glass pill shows the timer while you talk.

## What you get

- **Two tracks, one timeline.** Your microphone is always you; the system audio is everyone else.
- **Speakers** on the other side, detected on your Mac and renamed once per conversation.
- **Save locations** with their own folder, file name template, formats, audio handling and after-save actions.
- **Meeting detection** for Zoom, Teams, Google Meet in the browser, Slack, FaceTime and more.
- **A library** of every recording, searchable, with Transcribe Again and Save Again.
- **Audio import** for voice memos and old recordings.
