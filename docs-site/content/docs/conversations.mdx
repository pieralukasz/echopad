**Conversations** lists every recording and import, grouped by day, with its length, app and number of speakers. Search looks through titles, speaker names and the full text of transcripts.

<Screenshot name="conversations" alt="Conversations list and transcript" />

## A conversation

- **Speaker chips** under the title. Click one to rename, for example Speaker 1 to Anna. The files in your save location are updated too.
- **Open**, **Show in Finder** and **Copy Text** act on the saved transcript.
- **More** has:
  - **Save to**, to move the conversation to another save location and write its files there.
  - **Save Again**, to write the files once more.
  - **Transcribe Again**, for example after changing the language or turning on speaker detection.

Right-click a conversation in the list for **Show in Finder**, **Transcribe Again** and **Remove from EchoPad**. Removing deletes it from EchoPad’s library only. Files in your save locations stay.

## If something goes wrong

- A recording that fails to transcribe keeps its audio and shows **Transcribe Again**.
- If EchoPad quits or crashes while recording or transcribing, the tracks are kept and the transcription finishes the next time it opens.
