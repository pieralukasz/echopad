The first time EchoPad opens, a short setup window walks you through four steps.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Welcome

What EchoPad does, in one sentence.

</Step>
<Step>

### Permissions

- **Microphone** records your side of the conversation.
- **System Audio Recording** records what your Mac plays: the other people in a call, a video, a webinar.

Press **Allow…** next to each one. If you said no by accident, **Open Privacy Settings…** takes you to the right switch.

</Step>
<Step>

### Where to save

Pick a folder for transcripts. It becomes your first [save location](/docs/save-locations); you can add more later. Choose a folder inside an Obsidian vault and EchoPad notices, embeds audio with wiki links and can open the note in Obsidian.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ready

The models download in the background while you read this (about 480 MB, once). EchoPad now waits in the menu bar.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Your first recording

1. Click the EchoPad icon in the menu bar and press **Start Recording**, or press **⇧⌘E**.
2. Talk for a bit. The pill at the bottom of the screen shows the timer and a waveform.
3. Press the stop button on the pill, or **⇧⌘E** again.

A few seconds later the transcript is in your folder and in the **Conversations** list.
